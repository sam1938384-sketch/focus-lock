What this app can and can't do

Real: camera-guided zone placement, motion-sensor detection of the phone being picked up, a running timer, a 10-second alarm with a randomized unlock challenge, screen wake-lock while locked, and fullscreen to reduce accidental exits.

Browser limits: a web app cannot block your phone's back/home gestures, the app switcher, or the power button the way a native app with special permissions can. Switching apps or locking your screen will pause detection until you return here.

iOS note: Safari will ask for a one-time motion-sensor permission — tap "Enable camera" to trigger that prompt too.

Tip: for the strongest version of this, add this page to your home screen (share icon → Add to Home Screen) and open it from there — it runs full-screen with no browser chrome to accidentally tap.
